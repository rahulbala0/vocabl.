# 2–3 minute demo script

Keep the joystick in frame. Keyboard is the backup if BLE misbehaves.

1. **Problem (20s)**  
   “Communication boards make people spell letter by letter. A simple ‘I’m tired’ can take minutes. SpeakEasy is for nonverbal people with motor disabilities.”

2. **Setup (15s)**  
   Show the ESP32 + joystick. Chrome on the tablet. Hit Connect joystick. Yellow highlight moves.

3. **Live loop (60s)**  
   Partner: “Hey Alex, coffee or tea?”  
   Listen → four different replies appear (agree, decline, ask back, specific).  
   User flicks to a tile, clicks. Board speaks. Partner answers naturally. Repeat once (“How are you feeling?”).

4. **Access modes (25s)**  
   Categories → Food. Scanning mode: highlight walks, one click speaks. Custom phrases in Settings.

5. **Why AI / why this track (20s)**  
   Grok writes replies that fit this person and this moment. The joystick is the body; the tablet is the voice. Human connection stays in the room.

6. **Close (10s)**  
   “From minutes of spelling to seconds of conversation.”
